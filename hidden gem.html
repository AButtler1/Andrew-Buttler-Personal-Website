Hidden Gem

Casa Enrique

Casa Enrique is well known by myself and others who live in Long Island City. However, I find that its location outside of Manhattan has kept it some what secret and undiscovered by the foodie masses in NYC. Casa Enrique is an amazing Michellin star Mexican restaurant a few blocks from The Gantry State Park in Hunters Pointe Long Island City. If you ever find yourself in the area I highly recommend the lengua tacos they are amazing!
