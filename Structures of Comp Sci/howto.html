How To:

As a former reconnaissance expert in the 82nd Airborne Division I think there are many practical skills people should know.

Emergency medical skills are time critical and can save you or a loved ones life.

Applying a tourniquet in a hasty and effective manner can save someones life. Luckily the procedure is extremely simple. Understanding when it must be done is critical. A tourniquet is used to control arterial bleeding in a patient. Arterial bleeding can be identified by excessive amounts of blood that often sprays or pulses when it comes out of the patient. How does this happen to individuals in regular life?
  • Car Accident
  • Kitchen Injury
  • Working with power tools
Tourniquets are used on the extremeties of the body, to apply the tourniquet you must loosen the velcro closure and wrap your limb once above the injury. Think if your forearm was bleeding you would apply it to the injured arm above the injury but below the shoulder in order to apply effective pressure. With the free end of the tourniquet strap youll feed it through the plastic fastener and tighten it down as much as you possibly can. Then secure the strap with velcro. Next the tourniquet has a tightening bar, spin this bar multiple times and watch to see the bleeding stop at the point of injury. Now secure the bar in the plastic locking bar next to it. Ensure bleeding has stopped and now treat the wound with gauze or banadages as neccessary.
