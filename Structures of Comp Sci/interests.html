Interests

I am very interested in what the future for humanity will look like in terms of developments in physics, computing, and biomedical sciences. As I look forward to a career after Fordham I am drawn towards working in venture capital, private equity, and quantitative analysis in the stock market. These career paths would allow me to work in my areas of interest while being able to affect the future by investing in companies that matter.
